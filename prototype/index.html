Bullpen: learn to invest
Bullpen
Prototype v0.2 · working name

A clickable demo of the core loop for AUB student interviews.

  1. Pick your level, or take the placement test
  2. Play a lesson: wrong answers cost practice capital, right ones earn points
  3. In Trade, join a competition with code FINA-310, or create your own

Players and numbers are samples. Progress is kept in this browser only and never sent anywhere. "Restart the demo" on the Profile tab wipes it.

Margin call

You've run out of practice capital. In the app it refills over a few hours, or Pro gives you unlimited capital. For this demo you can refill now.
